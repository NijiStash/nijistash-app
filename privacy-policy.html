Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

NijiStash ("the app") is developed by MGB Studio. This policy explains what information the app accesses and what happens to it.

The short version

NijiStash does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data to us or to anyone else. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. Everything you save in the app stays on your phone, in the app's own private storage, until you delete it or share it yourself.

What the app accesses, and why

  • Camera — only when you choose to take a photo or record a video from inside the app's "+" menu. The result is saved directly into the app's private storage; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • Microphone — only when you choose to record an audio note. Recordings are saved the same way, on-device only.
  • Location — only when you choose to save your current location or pick a point on the map (the "Add location" option). The app reads your device's location at that moment to save a location item; it does not track or log your location in the background, and does not share it with any third party.
  • Files, links, images, videos, and contact cards (vCards) you share into the app — when you use Android's share sheet to send something to NijiStash, or pick a file from your device, a private copy is made inside the app's own storage so it keeps working even if the original is later moved or deleted. The app does not browse or access any other files on your device on its own.
  • Internet access — used only to fetch a preview (title/thumbnail) for a web link you paste into the app, and to check whether your device is online so the app can show an offline notice. No other data is sent over the network, and no data about you or your saved items is ever sent anywhere.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect analytics or usage statistics.
  • We don't have user accounts, logins, or a backend server.
  • We don't share, sell, or transmit your data to any third party.
  • We don't show ads.

Your data, your control

Everything you save lives in the app's private storage on your device. You can delete any item or collection at any time from within the app, and deleting the app itself removes all of its data from your device (unless you've backed it up yourself, e.g. via the app's own "Export" feature, which creates a .zip file you explicitly choose to save or share).

Children's privacy

NijiStash is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
